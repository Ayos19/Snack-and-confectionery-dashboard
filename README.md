## Conclusion

The dashboard covers 4,210 items across 922 brands, with $213M in revenue and 27.2M units of volume. Revenue grew 15.3% quarter over quarter and volume grew 12.8%.

### Key Findings
- **A few brands dominate.** Land Corp leads at about $40M, followed by Fusion Snacks ($21M) and Vista Goods ($19M).
- **Chips and biscuits are the core categories.** Chips earn about $97M and biscuits about $68M. Biscuits and chips also lead on volume.
- **Savory items win.** Crispy Cheddar is the top item by both revenue ($7.3M) and volume.
- **Bags are the best package type.** Bags earn about $98M and move 11.2M units, far ahead of every other package.
- **Small and specialty stores move more volume** than supermarket chains.

### Recommendations
1. Prioritize chips and biscuits in bag packaging.
2. Expand distribution through small and specialty stores.
3. Invest in the high-performing savory flavors.
4. Help brands outside the top 10 grow by copying what the leaders do.
